Upstream research and software

SHRED’s architecture originates in Williams, Zahn and Kutz, *Sensing with shallow recurrent decoder networks*, Proc. R. Soc. A (2024), DOI, with primary preprint.

Parametric SHRED-ROM is described by Tomasetto, Williams, Braghin, Manzoni and Kutz, *Reduced order modeling with shallow recurrent decoder networks*, Nature Communications 16, 10260 (2025), DOI.

The active implementation imports PySHRED. Local utils/models.py and utils/processdata.py correspond closely to SHRED-ROM. Function AST comparisons matched fit, forecast, and all inspected preprocessing/plotting helpers; class/import formatting differs. The exact historical upstream commit is unknown. Copies remain archived with upstream MIT notice, copyright 2024 Matteo Tomasetto – Stefano Riva. That upstream notice does not select a license for Andrea’s project.

OpenFOAM and ParaView provide simulation/export; PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, scikit-learn and Matplotlib provide learning and analysis.
