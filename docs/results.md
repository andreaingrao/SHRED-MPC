## Selection and metrics

Use `eligible_for_statistics == 1`: 210 candidate rows from cycles 8–49, corresponding to 42 decisions at controller timestep ≥250. Pressure relative L2 flattens 50 × 3,600 values; pressure-lift relative L2 uses 50 values. Historical denominator is `max(norm(reference),1e-12)`. All 250 archived ratios were recomputed; discrepancies were ≤2.23e−14 for pressure and ≤1.41e−8 for lift, consistent with archive precision. Eligible lift reference norms range 3.2511–10.6915. Pointwise relative error near zero is deliberately not featured; absolute errors remain available.

For pressure, median 3.05954%, Q1 2.38366%, Q3 3.66397%. For pressure lift, median 18.42603%, Q1 14.89390%, Q3 20.27132%. These pooled quartiles describe correlated candidates from one trajectory/model. They are not confidence intervals or independent-model uncertainty.

Select exactly one `shred_selected == 1` row per eligible cycle for decisions: 27/42 choices disagree with CFD’s minimum cost among the five tested endpoints. Median absolute CFD regret is 0.0193097, IQR 0–0.0513738, maximum 0.0857149. Near-tied candidates can disagree with small regret. No baseline closed-loop benefit or speedup follows from these numbers.
